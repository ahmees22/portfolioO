About me

I am a Mechatronics Technology graduate (2026) from Helwan International Technological University, based in Cairo. My core work is sheet-metal and mechanical design: enclosures, electrical panels, frames and brackets taken from 3D model to drawings, flat patterns and manufacturing packages.

Most of my designs are verified before they are built. I run FEA and CFD in ANSYS to check stresses, safety factors, airflow and heat, and I back the results with hand calculations.

I am always learning and keep up with new tools day by day. I use AI tools inside my engineering work to raise quality, cut delivery time and save money for the project. The method is shown below.

Sheet-metal design is my main focus

From an empty envelope to parts the workshop can cut and bend the same day. This is the method I follow on every enclosure and panel, shown with real numbers from my 4000 A distribution board.

73sheet-metal parts in one panel

1

Envelope & requirements

Fix the outer size, the equipment inside, IP rating, loads and service access before any part is drawn.

2

Material & thickness

2.5 mm for frame profiles, 1.5–2 mm for covers and doors, galvanised or pre-painted sheet, non-magnetic plate where cables of one phase pass.

3

Bends & flanges

Inner radius, flange length, bend reliefs and stiffeners (hat sections on tall doors) so parts bend cleanly and stay flat.

4

Joining & fixings

Welded frame where squareness matters; rivet nuts, thread-forming screws and slotted holes so every panel can be removed for service.

5

Flat blanks & files

Flat pattern and bend table for every part, 1:1 DXF / DWG with CUT, BEND and ETCH layers, STEP for the formed part.

6

Drawings, BOM & review

Part drawings with hole schedules, BOM with mass, then a design review: earthing continuity, galvanising vents, hinge and cut-out checks.

How I use AI in engineering work

AI handles the repetitive parts, so I can spend my time on design decisions and checking. Every result is still verified by engineering judgement and hand calculations.

↑

Higher quality

AI-assisted review of drawings, BOMs and reports catches missing dimensions, unit errors and inconsistent part numbers before release. Simulation results are cross-checked with hand calculations.

⏱

Faster delivery

Scripted, parametric CAD and simulation (SolidWorks macros, Python, ANSYS journals) builds part families and repeats studies in minutes instead of rebuilding them by hand.

$

Lower project cost

Design options are compared and optimised digitally (topology optimisation, revision A vs B, PSO), so material is saved and fewer physical prototypes are needed.

Requirement→Parametric 3D model→FEA / CFD check→AI-assisted review→Drawings, flat patterns, BOM→Manufacturing
